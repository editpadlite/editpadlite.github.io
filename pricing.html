EditPad Lite pricing: free for personal use, 24.95 euro for work

EditPad Lite pricing is simple: nothing for personal use, 24.95 euro per user for work, and volume packages up to 100 users. These figures come from the vendor's purchase page and exclude tax.

EditPad Lite pricing starts at zero for home and school use that earns no income. A single user licence for work costs 24.95 euro before tax, a 5 user package is 99.95 euro, and the price per user falls to 8.99 euro at 100 users. Upgrades from version 7 cost 12.95 euro, and every purchase has a three month money back guarantee.

How many licences you need

Licences are counted by user, and the rule is more generous than a count per computer. The size you need is the smaller of two numbers: the people who use EditPad Lite and the computers it runs on. Five people with ten computers need a 5 user licence, and nine people sharing four computers need a 4 user licence.

One person can install a single user licence on every computer they use. One computer shared by any number of people is also covered by a single user licence. On servers, the count is the people and client computers that reach the program, and the number of servers does not matter.

None of this applies at home. If your use is personal, the free edition rules cover you and there is nothing to buy.

What the price includes

The licence is a single purchase for the major version you buy. The vendor's upgrade policy says a later major version usually costs about half the product price, and that nobody is forced to upgrade: the version you bought stays available.

Payment is by credit, debit or prepaid card through a secure checkout, or by bank transfer. Companies with an approval process can request a formal quote, which is shown in the browser at once. With a card, the download is available right after checkout.

FAQ

How much does EditPad Lite cost?

EditPad Lite is free for personal use. For work or business use, a single user licence costs 24.95 euro before tax. Volume packages lower the price per user, down to 8.99 euro each in the 100 user package.

Is EditPad Lite a subscription?

No recurring plan is listed on the vendor's purchase page. A licence is a single purchase that covers every release of the major version you bought, such as all 8.x.x builds. A later major version is an optional paid upgrade, usually at about half price.

Is there an EditPad Lite coupon or discount code?

The vendor publishes no coupon codes on its purchase, upgrade or guarantee pages. Its published discounts are volume pricing, a 12.95 euro upgrade price for owners of older versions and a bundle with AceText at 49.95 euro.

Do I need one EditPad Lite licence per computer?

No. Licences are counted by users, and the number you need is the smaller of the people using the program and the computers it runs on. One person may install a single user licence on all the computers they use.

Are taxes included in the EditPad Lite price?

No. The listed prices exclude sales tax, VAT or GST, which is added at checkout in countries that charge it on internet sales. Tax exempt businesses can enter a tax ID or VAT number at checkout to remove it.

How can I pay for EditPad Lite?

The vendor's checkout accepts major credit cards, debit cards and prepaid cards, and bank transfer is also offered. A formal quote can be requested for company purchasing processes. With card payment the download is available immediately after checkout.
