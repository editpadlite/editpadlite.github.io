What the guarantee says

The EditPad Lite refund policy is short. If you are not entirely happy with your purchase for any reason, you can ask for your money back within three months. The vendor calls the guarantee unconditional and lists no exclusions on its guarantee page. It applies to EditPad Lite and to the vendor's other products.

The guarantee has one built in step: the vendor first tries to fix what bothers you. Its page says that if it cannot improve EditPad Lite or solve the problem to your full satisfaction, it will promptly issue a full refund.

The steps below follow the vendor's guarantee page. Keep your order email at hand, since it identifies the purchase.

Updates and upgrades after you buy

A licence covers every release with the same first version number, so an 8.x.x licence includes all 8.x.x updates. Help > Check for New Version shows whether a newer build exists, and running the new installer is enough to update.

A major upgrade, such as a future 9.0.0, usually costs about half the product price. It is free if released within twelve months of your purchase, and the vendor says nobody is forced to upgrade: the older version stays available and supported, without new features.

The money back guarantee covers upgrade purchases too. Moving to the larger edition is a separate purchase, described in the Lite versus Pro comparison.

Frequently asked questions

Does EditPad Lite have a money back guarantee?

Yes. The vendor offers a three month unconditional money back guarantee on EditPad Lite and its other products. It applies if you are not entirely happy with the purchase for any reason.

How do I get a refund for EditPad Lite?

Uninstall EditPad Lite and email the vendor through the contact page on its site. The vendor first tries to solve the problem. If it cannot do so to your satisfaction, it states that it will promptly issue a full refund.

How long does an EditPad Lite refund take?

The vendor does not state a processing time. Its guarantee page says only that a full refund is issued promptly once it is clear the problem cannot be solved.

Do I need to cancel an EditPad Lite subscription?

No subscription is listed on the vendor's purchase page. A licence is a single purchase for one major version, so nothing renews. A later major version is an optional paid upgrade.

Are EditPad Lite upgrades covered by the guarantee?

Yes. The vendor's upgrade policy says the guarantee also covers upgrade purchases. A major upgrade released within twelve months of your purchase is free in any case.

Can I keep using EditPad Lite after a refund?

Not under the paid licence. The guarantee procedure starts with uninstalling the program, and a refund ends the licence that allowed work use. Personal use at home or school remains free under the vendor's standard terms.
