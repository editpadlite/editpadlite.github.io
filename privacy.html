Data collected

This is a static website with no accounts, forms, comments or cookies of its own. The hosting provider may keep standard request logs (IP address, user agent, time) to serve pages and prevent abuse.

If a privacy friendly analytics script is added later, it will count page views in aggregate only. No personal profiles are built.

Affiliate links

Outbound links marked as sponsored go to the vendor or an affiliate network. Those sites set their own cookies to attribute a purchase, and their privacy policies apply there. We receive aggregated commission reports, not your personal data.

Third party requests

Pages load a web font from Google Fonts. Google receives the request for the font file, including your IP address.

Pages use Google Analytics to count visits and clicks on outbound links. Google sets its own cookies for this and its privacy policy applies. Page view and click events are sent with the page address and the name of the link clicked, not with your name or email.

Your rights

Because no personal data is held, there is nothing to export or delete on our side. Questions: see the About page. Last updated 2026-11-05.
