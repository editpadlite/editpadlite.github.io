EditPad Lite vs EditPad Pro: what the extra 25 euro buys

EditPad Lite vs EditPad Pro is a choice between a free, smaller editor and its full sibling from the same vendor. Both share one user's guide and one look. Pro adds the power tools and removes the file size ceiling.

EditPad Lite and EditPad Pro are two editions of the same Windows editor from Just Great Software. Lite is free for personal use or 24.95 euro per user for work, and covers tabs, Unicode, regular expression search and syntax colouring. Pro starts at 49.95 euro and adds column selection, macros, file comparison, a hex editor, FTP, projects and support for files larger than memory.

The short version

EditPad Lite vs EditPad Pro is less a contest than a ladder. The two editions share a look and a single user's guide that marks the Pro only features. Version 8.6.1 of both was released on 8 April 2026, and both run on Windows XP through Windows 11 in 32 bit and 64 bit.

Lite is the part of the editor most people need: open files in tabs, edit in any encoding, search with regular expressions, undo without limit. Pro is aimed at people who work in text all day and need to reshape it, compare it, automate it or reach it on a server.

When Lite is enough

Lite covers the job if your files are ordinary text, logs under 2 GB, configuration files or small scripts, and your editing is typing, searching and replacing. Its encoding support is the same as in Pro, so nothing is lost when you open a mainframe export or a file with mixed line breaks. For home use it costs nothing, which the free versus licensed page explains.

Lite is also the lighter download: 18 MB against 24 MB for Pro on the vendor's download pages. The review of EditPad Lite covers its feature set in more depth.

When Pro is the better buy

If two or more of these describe a normal week, the 25 euro difference is small. If none do, Lite will not hold you back. People who need such tools without paying can also look at the free alternatives, one of which lists a macro recorder and a text compare tool.

  • You edit tables or fixed width data and need column selections.
  • You clean lists and want sorting, trimming of trailing spaces and case conversion.
  • You repeat the same edits and would record them as a macro.
  • You compare versions of a file.
  • You follow growing log files with the tail command.
  • You edit files on a server over FTP or SFTP.
  • Your files exceed available memory.

Trying Pro and upgrading later

Pro has a free evaluation download. According to the vendor's download page it includes every feature except the spell checker and configurable print headers, and it reminds you to buy a licence if you keep using it. The page states no trial length in days.

Buyers of EditPad Lite can move to Pro for 29.95 euro where new customers pay 49.95 euro, by entering their Lite user ID and email address. The discount applies only when buying direct from the vendor. The Lite licence stays valid afterwards and both editors can run on the same computer. FAQ

What is the difference between EditPad Lite and EditPad Pro?

Both are editions of the same Windows text editor. Lite covers tabs, Unicode, regular expression search and syntax colouring. Pro adds column selections, sorting, bookmarks, macros, a file compare tool, a hex editor, projects, an FTP client and support for files larger than memory.

Is EditPad Pro free?

No. EditPad Pro starts at 49.95 euro for a single user, and the vendor offers a free evaluation download that reminds you to buy a licence. Only EditPad Lite is free, and only for personal use.

Can I upgrade from EditPad Lite to EditPad Pro?

Yes. People who bought EditPad Lite can buy EditPad Pro for 29.95 euro where new customers pay 49.95 euro, using their Lite user ID and email address. The discount applies only when buying direct from the vendor, and the Lite licence remains valid.

Does EditPad Lite have column mode?

No. The vendor lists rectangular selections, also called column selections, as an EditPad Pro feature. EditPad Lite works with ordinary selections only.

Can I run EditPad Lite and EditPad Pro on the same computer?

Yes. The vendor states that after upgrading, the EditPad Lite licence stays valid and both programs can run on the same computer at once.

Which edition handles larger files?

EditPad Pro. The free edition of Lite opens files up to 2 GB and a licensed Lite copy opens files that fit in available memory. Pro edits files over 4 GB, including files larger than the computer's memory.
